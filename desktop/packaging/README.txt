AirCursor - control your computer with your hand
================================================

Linux
  1. Run ./install.sh once (cursor permission + app-menu entry; asks for sudo).
  2. Start AirCursor from your app menu, or run ./aircursor
     Windows: double-click aircursor.exe

  Raise your index finger and move. Pinch to click. Press Space to pause.
  Pause/quit is always one click away on the floating panel.
  From a shortcut:  ./aircursor ctl toggle     (pause / resume)     ./aircursor ctl quit

Nothing to install: Python, the hand-tracking model and all libraries are inside this folder.
Video never leaves your computer.

Full guide, gestures and troubleshooting: https://github.com/Sanjarbek-2007/airmouse
